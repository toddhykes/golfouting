📱 SMS Privacy & Terms

🛡️ Your Privacy is Protected

We only use your phone number to send you updates about your current golf outing. No marketing, no sharing, no nonsense.

📞 What We Text You About

  • Event updates, schedules, and tee times
  • Live scoring updates during your round
  • Final results and leaderboards
  • Important announcements about your outing

🔒 How We Protect Your Information

  • No sharing: Your phone number stays private and is never shared with third parties
  • No marketing: We will never send promotional or marketing messages
  • Event-specific: Your number is only used for the golf outing you signed up for
  • Secure storage: All data is encrypted and stored securely

✋ Your Control

  • Opt out anytime: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately
  • Get help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance
  • Re-subscribe: Reply START to opt back in if you change your mind
  • Event organizer: Contact your organizer directly to update your preferences

💰 Costs & Frequency

  • Message rates: Standard message and data rates may apply from your carrier
  • Frequency: Typically 1-5 messages per golf outing
  • Free service: This golf tracking service itself is free to use

📋 Terms Summary

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive text messages about your golf outing. Messages are sent via automated system. Consent is not required to participate in the golf outing. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.
